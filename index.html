What is Valorant?

VALORANT is a shooter FPS game created by Riot Games, "tactical shooter" they say. It's a 5v5 with abilities that can make damage to your enemies or helping you take control, flank/lurk, kill, defend, etc. All of them depends on the role you're taking.

There are 4 roles: controller, centinel, duelist and initiator. Each one with their own responsabilities.

Ranks

This is a controversial theme. It wasn't a long time ago when there only exist 8 ranks but then Riot decided we needed a ninth rank: ascendant. This is a rank created to make the jump from diamond to inmortal 'cause it's between them.
